The path

I wasn't supposed to become a designer.

2020 – 2024

Tried to want to code.

B.Tech in Computer Science. I practised coding and realised I didn't want to spend my life on it. A colleague mentioned UI/UX, and I started with the tool, Figma: tutorials, recreating designs, breaking things, trying again.

Jun – Jul 2024

First break.

A month of remote design at Codefast Technology: a restaurant reservation app, an e-learning platform, landing pages, posters and logos. Then a move to another state wasn't realistic on the pay, and the advice was to look in Bangalore.

Jul 2024 – Jul 2025

Bangalore, within a week.

At Lazy Idli I designed PlayOS (a sports booking app and dashboard for PlayArena), the Project Teho website, and CricketOS. I also turned F1 race tracks into files for a wood-etching machine. I rarely knew what was next, and that was the learning.

Jul 2025 – now

Wanting more, then the twist.

Relanto started in marketing: decks, graphics, demo videos. I wondered if I'd chosen wrong. Then came client and product work, including a confidential Google engagement, and AI and enterprise products like SmartQuote and RelPay.

Next

Still becoming.

What I'm missing is a senior designer beside me who says "here's why this doesn't work." I'm looking for a team with strong design mentorship, where I can take on harder problems.
